1. About This Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Barboza Bru Corp, the company responsible for The Barboza Method brand, uses cookies and similar technologies on its websites.

Our goal is to provide clear information about these technologies, their purposes, and the choices available to visitors.

Contact: barbozamethodacademy@gmail.com

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files or identifiers stored on or accessed from a visitor's device while browsing a website.

They may support essential website functions, remember preferences, analyze website usage, and assist advertising activities.

Similar technologies, including tracking pixels and digital identifiers, may also be used for these purposes.

3. Categories of Cookies

Essential Cookies

These support essential functionality, security, and the storage of privacy preferences. Where permitted by applicable law, strictly necessary cookies do not require consent.

Analytics Cookies

These help us understand how visitors use our websites, including visits, page views, and interactions. Google Analytics 4 may be used for these purposes.

Advertising Cookies

These support advertising measurement and, where permitted, remarketing and targeted advertising. Technologies may include the Meta Pixel and other appropriately disclosed tools.

4. Consent and Preferences

Where required by applicable law, optional analytics and advertising technologies will only be activated after the visitor provides consent.

Visitors may accept or reject optional cookies through the website's cookie banner.

Visitors may also review or withdraw their choices using the cookie preferences control.

Withdrawing consent will stop future processing based on that consent, but it does not automatically reverse processing that was lawfully performed before withdrawal.

5. Third-Party Technologies

Third-party providers, including Google Analytics and Meta, may use their own cookies or identifiers.

When visitors access external services, such as the Hotmart checkout, those providers may use additional technologies governed by their own privacy policies.

We encourage visitors to review the relevant third-party notices.

6. Cookie Duration

Cookie retention periods vary depending on their purpose, configuration, and provider.

Some cookies operate during a browsing session, while others may remain on a device for longer periods.

We will periodically review the technologies actually in use and update this Policy when necessary.

7. Browser Controls

In addition to the controls provided on our websites, visitors may configure their browsers to block or delete cookies.

Blocking certain essential technologies may affect specific website features.

8. Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in technologies, processing purposes, or applicable legal requirements.
