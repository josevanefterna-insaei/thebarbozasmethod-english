FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

BEFORE YOU START

Do I need martial arts experience?

No. The Foundation level starts with stance, movement and essential striking mechanics. Experienced athletes can choose the level that best matches their current training.

Can I train at home?

Yes. Many lessons can be practiced with open space and basic equipment. A heavy bag or training partner adds more options, especially in the advanced routines.

Is the course in English?

Yes. The U.S. edition is fully accessible in English, with English-language lesson support throughout the program.

How long do I have access?

Every course purchase includes 12 months of access. This applies whether you purchase Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced individually, or choose the Complete System with all three levels and its exclusive bonuses.

Is Barboza Fight Club included with the Complete System?

The Barboza Fight Club is a separate membership. Complete System students can activate a 30-day free trial after purchase. After the trial, the membership continues under the selected subscription plan unless canceled. Your 12-month access to the Complete System is independent of the Fight Club membership.

What if the program is not right for me?

You may request a refund within seven days of purchase, according to the checkout platform's guarantee terms.
